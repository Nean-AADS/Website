Business Publication Services

Required notices. Coordinated from placement to proof.

Some jurisdictions require newspaper notices for business formations, registrations to do business in another state, or assumed names (DBAs). Requirements vary by entity type and location, including eligible newspapers, publication schedules, deadlines and any required follow-up filings.

AADS coordinates the publication process—from confirming the applicable requirements and arranging newspaper placement to obtaining publication affidavits and coordinating any required certificate filings. Get the notices and supporting documentation your business needs through one point of contact, with scope, fees and anticipated timing confirmed before work begins.

Answers for Your Next Request

What corporate compliance services does AADS support?

AADS supports certificates of good standing, corporate record retrieval, plain and certified copies, filing submissions under authorized instructions, and registered agent coordination. The requested work and jurisdiction requirements are confirmed before proceeding.

Can AADS help obtain certificates of good standing?

Yes. Provide the exact legal entity name, issuing state, file number if known, recipient requirements and date needed. AADS coordinates the certificate of good standing or the equivalent certificate offered by that issuing office.

Is a certificate of good standing the same as a certified copy?

No. A certificate of good standing addresses entity status as defined by the issuing office. A certified copy reproduces a specified filed document with that office’s certification. Provide the receiving party’s exact requirement.

Can I request foreign qualification, annual reports or entity changes?

Yes. Identify the entity, formation jurisdiction, state where the filing is needed and the requested action. Provide the file number if known, authorized instructions, supporting documents and deadline. AADS confirms the filing requirements, fees and anticipated timing for that request.
