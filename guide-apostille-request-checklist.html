What to Provide Before Requesting an Apostille or Authentication

REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 9 NOVEMBER 2026

Start here

Identify the document’s origin, destination country and receiving party’s instructions before choosing a processing route. The word “apostille” alone does not define the whole request.

Start with origin and destination

USAGov explains that apostilles and authentication certificates verify signatures, stamps or seals on documents, and that the destination affects the required route. Its guidance distinguishes state-issued records from federal records and directs users to the relevant authorities. The U.S. Department of State also publishes separate preparation and submission guidance for authentication and apostille requests.

These distinctions make a complete intake important. A document issued by a state authority and one issued by a federal authority should not be routed on the assumption that the same office handles both.

The information to collect

  • Document type: identify the actual record, such as a corporate document, vital record, court record or other specified document.
  • Issuing authority: state who issued or certified it and the state or federal origin. Supply a clear copy for route review when appropriate.
  • Country of use: name the destination and provide the receiving party’s written instructions.
  • Available version: identify whether you hold an original, certified copy, photocopy or electronic record.
  • Additional requirements: include any translation, issuance-date, original-format or consular requirement stated by the recipient.
  • Timing and delivery: give the date needed, delivery destination and any deadline dependency.

Do not change the document before review

Avoid adding a notarization, detaching a certification or mailing an irreplaceable original solely because a generic checklist suggests it. Preparation requirements depend on the document and authority. Confirm what is needed for this specific request before altering or submitting the record.

Illustrative request wording

“Please review the attached document copy and receiving-party instructions for use in the country identified below. Confirm the required document version, processing authorities, anticipated timing, fees and return-delivery arrangements before we send the original.”

What should the confirmation cover?

Keep a written record of the agreed route, what the client supplies, any documents to be retrieved, and what is being returned. If recipient requirements remain uncertain, identify the open question rather than promising acceptance. AADS can coordinate the confirmed processing work; the issuing and receiving authorities retain their respective roles.

What does an apostille verify?

It relates to the signature, seal or official capacity associated with the public document. It should not be represented as validating every factual assertion in that document.
