Prepare Your Request

  • Exact party or entity names; identify additional names and related entities separately.
  • States, counties, courts, search period and the documents your reviewing team needs.
  • Any required certifications, copies, page limits and recipient instructions.
  • Matter reference, responsible reviewer and date needed.

Example Request

“For [matter], please quote UCC and lien research for [entity/name] in [states/counties], civil litigation in [courts], bankruptcy within [scope], and a certificate of good standing from [state]. Include [specified copies] and identify any separate components, fees or source limits.”

What the Handoff Should Make Clear

The record package should make it clear what was searched, what was located and which documents remain outstanding. AADS supplies factual research and authorized filing support; your team makes the legal judgments about scope and effect.

Answers for Your Request

Can related requests use one matter reference?

Yes. Identify the matter reference and each subject, jurisdiction and deliverable separately. The confirmed scope can coordinate the related research and document work through one point of contact.

Can I request a particular complaint, order or judgment?

Yes. Provide the court, case number and docket entry or document description if known. Identify certification and copy requirements; availability and any copy charges are confirmed for the request.
