Prepare Your Request

  • Exact legal entity name, entity type, formation state and file number if known.
  • The receiving party’s document wording and any issuance-date requirement.
  • Plain or certified copies, requested filings and authorized instructions.
  • Destination country for international use, client reference and deadline.

Example Request

“For [entity] formed in [state], please obtain the certificate of good standing required by [recipient], issued within [required period], and a certified copy of [specific filing]. The documents will be used in [country]. Please confirm the certification route, fees and anticipated timing.”

What the Handoff Should Make Clear

An online status result, official certificate of good standing and certified copy are distinct deliverables. AADS coordinates the requested factual records and authorized submissions. Tax, legal-structure and continuing compliance decisions remain with the client’s advisers.

Answers for Your Request

Can AADS obtain a certificate of good standing for my client?

Yes. Provide the exact entity name, issuing jurisdiction, file number if known, recipient wording and date needed. The issuing office determines the available certificate and its stated meaning.

Can document retrieval and international certification be coordinated?

Yes. Identify the document, issuing authority, destination country and receiving party’s requirements. AADS confirms the retrieval and certification steps for that request.
