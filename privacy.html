How All American Document Services collects, uses and protects the information you send through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by All American Document Services, LLC (“AADS”, “we”, “us”). This notice explains what information we collect when you use the Contact form or the Place an Order form, why we collect it and how we protect it.

Information you give us

When you send a form, we receive the details you enter. Depending on the request, this can include:

  • your name, company, email address, phone number and billing details;
  • the service you need, names of companies, individuals or properties to be researched, jurisdictions, dates and instructions;
  • for criminal record research on individuals, the names, dates of birth and aliases you provide; and
  • any documents you choose to attach.

Please only send the information needed for your request. Do not include Social Security numbers, account passwords or similar sensitive identifiers unless we specifically ask for them through a secure channel.

Information collected automatically

To protect the forms from spam and abuse, we record the time a form was opened and submitted, your browser type, and a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address. We do not store your raw IP address with your request. [If enabled:] We use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a form is being sent by a person; Cloudflare processes limited technical information for this check under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • to respond to your inquiry and confirm scope, fees and timing;
  • to carry out, deliver and bill for the work you request;
  • to keep a record of requests received and the consent given; and
  • to protect our website and clients from fraud, spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use form submissions for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to complete your request, for example with public record offices, courts, filing offices, couriers or other service providers who help us do the work, and with technology providers that host our website and form records under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How we store and protect it

Form submissions and attachments are stored in a private database and private file storage operated by our hosting provider (Supabase). They are not publicly accessible and are available only to authorized AADS personnel. Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for [retention period to be confirmed], or longer where needed for an active matter, billing, or legal and regulatory requirements. Spam and abandoned submissions are deleted [timeframe to be confirmed].

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us, subject to any legal or contractual obligations to keep it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
