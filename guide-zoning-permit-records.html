REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

Start Here

Identify the property, responsible authority and exact document or question. Zoning responses, occupancy records and permit copies are separate requests.

Identify the Correct Authority

Provide the address, parcel number, city and county. Identify the department or jurisdiction already known to be responsible. A mailing address alone may not identify the office that holds zoning or building records.

For a Zoning Verification Letter

State the current or intended use and the specific questions the recipient wants answered. Supply the recipient’s wording or checklist when available. Do not assume a general zoning letter answers every question about a proposed use, variances or compliance.

For Occupancy or Permit Records

Identify whether you need an existing certificate of occupancy, permit copy, inspection record or another named document. Provide permit numbers, construction dates and prior addresses when known. Ask the authority to identify unavailable documents rather than assuming every building has the same records.

A Hillsborough County Example

Available records and retrieval procedures are confirmed with the responsible jurisdiction.

A Useful Request

“For [address/parcel], please obtain [specific letter or existing record]. The intended use is [use]; the recipient needs answers to [questions]. Known permits or dates are [details]. Please confirm the responsible authority, fees, anticipated timing and any record limits.”

Describe the Deliverable Accurately

An existing occupancy certificate or permit copy is evidence from the issuing authority’s records. Obtaining the copy does not independently approve a new use or certify all work at the property. Keep any legal or technical assessment with the client’s advisers.
