If obtaining quality public records research in Georgia has proved to be a challenge for your organization, you’re not alone. Georgia public records research companies have unfortunately earned a reputation for failing to provide the caliber of information their clients require in a timely manner. That’s why our team of trusted research experts is filling the gap with cost-effective, boots-on-the-ground records research solutions.

Our research process provides reliable, accurate records with predictable, dependable turnover times to meet the needs of your organization. You’ll have the information you need, regardless of your timelines or volume of work. Here’s how AADS’ boots on the ground researchers make public records research in Georgia easy.

Full Compliance

Our reports comply with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) as well as secure disposition of personal information laws. Our researchers perform primary research in-person visiting the proper clerk’s offices as well as the courts in Georgia in hand with state-of-the-art software. This provides you with the most accurate information. Turnaround times that usually take 48-72 hours are reduced to as little as 24.

Dependable Background Checks

Whether you require background checks for individuals, businesses, or assets in Georgia, you won’t run into the challenges research companies encounter using online systems or third-party databases. We avoid issues because our team uses screening solutions that are quicker. You’ll have the information you need to make informed decisions, using cost-effective background checks so you can avoid mistakes that will negatively impact your organization. We combine nationwide background checks with local searches, so you have the full story.

Official Data Searches

Our Public Records Searches in Georgia use official data sources to access public records. You can get quick reports based on records such as:

  • Full Name
  • Age
  • Recent Address
  • Phone Numbers
  • Address History
  • AKAs/Maiden Name
  • Potential Associates
  • Potential Relatives
  • Neighbors
  • Properties
  • Home Value
  • Aircraft / Boats
  • Hunting / Fishing License
  • Criminal Records
  • Concealed Weapon
  • Bankruptcies
  • Tax Liens
  • Judgments
  • Accident History
  • Death Record
  • Email Address

Full Criminal History Checks

Criminal histories come from different sources. We do a full criminal history check, so nothing is missed including:

  • County Criminal courthouse to search records for any felony, misdemeanor or traffic offense.
  • Federal Criminal searches at the federal courthouse in the designated search jurisdiction.
  • Statewide Criminal research to find felony and misdemeanor criminal incidents in the designated state.
  • Sex Offender Registry to find listed sex offenders.

Verification Reports for Employment 

We understand how easy it is for potential new hires to misrepresent themselves on their resumes. We will confirm all information provided including references and professional licenses as well as:

  • Driving Records (DMV)
  • Commercial Drivers Record Search
  • Education Verification
  • Employment Verification
  • Personal & Business Reference Checks
  • Professional License Verifications
  • Social Security Trace

Efficient Property Searches

We address all aspects of property searches to capture multiple records on real estate transactions including:

  • Real estate, tax, and deed records
  • Property description
  • Land description
  • Market and assessed value
  • Sale price
  • Current property owners
  • Outstanding liens on the property
  • Anything that can cloud title and affect ownership

Business Searches

You can make smarter, more profitable decisions with the right information whether you’re selecting business partners, suppliers, or customers. Our thorough business searches cover:

  • Bankruptcies
  • Judgments
  • Lawsuits
  • Liens
  • Corporate records
  • Assets
  • Executive names
  • Business addresses

Reputation Management

Negative news and Social Media Searches help to manage your digital reputation. We uncover potentially damaging information that can put you at legal or financial risk.

Patriot Act / OFAC Search

Without proper research, business, real estate, financial, or insurance-related transactions can unknowingly violate the Patriot Act and/or OFAC Regulations. We will ensure you are not supporting or conducting business with a sanctioned individual or institution to avoid severe civil and criminal penalties.

Clarifying Legal & Financial Risk with AADS

AADS is a national public records research company. We have become a top choice for due diligence, document filing/retrieval, service of process, and background checks in Georgia.

We support the title insurance and lending industry with public records research and retrieval and provide title abstracts, legal due diligence, courthouse retrieval, and many different searches, including municipal lien searches, estoppel, UCC searches, and corporate searches for law firms, businesses, real estate agents, and private investigators.

For more information on how we can help you reduce legal and financial risk, please visit our homepage or contact us today!
