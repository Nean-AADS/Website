Answers for Your Next Request

Is continuation tracking the same as filing a continuation?

No. Tracking and reminders identify dates and required follow-up. Filing requires authorized instructions and confirmation of the office’s response. The agreed arrangement must identify who is responsible for each step.

Does monitoring establish or guarantee lien priority?

No. Defined record checks can identify filings or other changes for review. Your legal or credit team determines their relevance and effect on lien position. Source coverage, timing and indexing limits remain part of the scope.

Does discussing monitoring enroll my filings or start alerts?

No. Scope, source availability, frequency, recipients, fees and responsibility must be confirmed before enrollment. This website does not currently operate a live monitoring subscription or automated alert service.
