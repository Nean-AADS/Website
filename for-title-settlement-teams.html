Prepare Your Request

  • Property address, parcel identifier, county and municipality if known.
  • Current owner and legal description where available.
  • Title depth, municipal components, utility providers and requested copies.
  • Closing date, client matter reference and separate recording instructions.

Example Request

“For [address/parcel] in [county/state], please quote [title depth] with [copies]. Separately identify municipal code, open-permit, utility and assessment responses, plus [tax years]. The target closing date is [date]. Please confirm the responsible offices and any outstanding source responses.”

What the Handoff Should Make Clear

A recorded-document search and a response from a utility or code office are separate research paths. The deliverable should identify each requested component, the responding source and unresolved items. AADS supplies factual records for your title and legal review.

Answers for Your Request

Does title research automatically include municipal responses?

No. Identify the municipal components separately and confirm the departments or providers responsible for the property. The confirmed request states which records and responses are included.

Can recording work be coordinated with the research?

Yes, under separate confirmed recording instructions. Identify the instrument, jurisdiction, required originals or electronic format and date needed.
