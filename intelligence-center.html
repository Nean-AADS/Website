How to use these guides

Each guide identifies its official sources and source-check date. State and local examples are labeled so a jurisdiction-specific process is not silently presented as a nationwide rule. Confirm current requirements, the requested scope and source availability for your actual matter.

Use these guides to prepare clearly defined research and document requests. Your reviewing professionals assess the legal and title implications.
