Zoning Letters, Occupancy and Permit Records

Obtain the specific local-authority response or document your review requires. Requests can include zoning verification letters, existing certificates of occupancy and permit copies, subject to the responsible authority’s records and procedures.

Identify the intended use and questions for a zoning letter. For occupancy or permit copies, identify the building, permit numbers and relevant dates when known. Retrieval of a record is different from a determination that a proposed use or construction activity is lawful.

Answers for Your Next Request

What is included in the Real Estate Package?

The package brings together a current owner title search, recording of documents, and municipal lien searches through the responsible city, county or other local authority. Confirm the property, search depth, municipal components and recording instructions for your quote.

Does a title search include every municipal record?

Recorded land records and local departmental records are separate research paths. Identify the municipal components required, such as code, permits, utilities or assessments, and confirm the responsible departments or providers for the property.

Does AADS provide a legal or title opinion?

AADS provides factual research, document retrieval and filing support. The client’s legal or title professionals assess the legal effect of the records. Research scope, sources and outstanding items are identified with the agreed work.

Which property search depth should I request?

Specify a current-owner search, two-owner search or a defined period or chain of records. State the records, tax information and supporting copies required. AADS confirms the requested coverage; your legal or title team selects the depth appropriate to the matter.

What does a legal and vesting deed report contain?

The agreed report identifies the owner names and vesting language shown in the recorded deed, its recording reference and the property’s legal description. It is a focused record report. Request lien, mortgage, tax or broader chain research separately where needed.

What is included in an assignment-record request?

Identify the mortgage or deed of trust and request the recorded assignments and related modifications or releases within the agreed sources and period. The research can list located instruments and apparent gaps for review; it does not decide ownership of a loan or legal enforceability.

What is needed for a zoning letter, certificate of occupancy or permit-copy request?

Provide the address, parcel identifier, responsible city or county if known, exact document requested and date needed. For zoning, include the intended use and questions for the authority. For occupancy and permit records, include known permit numbers, building dates or prior addresses. Availability depends on the authority’s records.
