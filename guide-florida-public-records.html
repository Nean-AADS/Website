Florida Public-Record Research: UCC, Property and Municipal Requests

Separate Florida’s central UCC registry, county recorded instruments and local departmental responses when preparing a transaction-research request.

STATE REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

Start with the Source Map

Use this guide to prepare the request. It maps source types and questions to confirm; it is not a statewide completeness certificate or a legal determination of required scope.

Keep the Central and County Requests Distinct

Florida’s Department of State directs UCC forms, filings, searches, copies and certification requests to its designated registry. That central UCC request should not be used as a label for every property or county-record inquiry. Identify fixture or real-property record work separately when instructed.

Identify the County and the Local Responders

Flagler County’s official-records description includes deeds, mortgages, assignments, liens and other instruments. Hillsborough County’s building-record guidance uses separate retrieval routes and date ranges for permits and occupancy records. These illustrate different sources; they do not define a standard package for every Florida property.

Ask for the Actual Documents

State whether you need a UCC search report, a certified search response, document images, a recorded instrument or a departmental response. For a certificate of occupancy or permit, provide the specific document type, property identifiers and known permit details. Older or unavailable records may need direct follow-up.

Request Checklist

  • Exact subject names and the requested central UCC search and copy requirements.
  • Property address, parcel identifier, county, owner period and requested title depth.
  • Municipal components and known city, county or utility providers.
  • Named courts or federal districts and any docket or certified-copy requirements.
  • Closing or matter deadline, delivery requirements and separately confirmed fees.

Example Request

“For [entity] and [Florida property], please quote the central UCC search with [certification/copies], the requested county property research covering [depth], and separate [code/permit/utility/assessment] inquiries. Identify the responsible offices, search through dates and outstanding responses. Our deadline is [date].”

Make the Result Assessable

Ask the deliverable to identify the exact names and property, sources and criteria used, covered period or through date, located references, supporting copies and any outstanding items. Online coverage, authentication, historical records and source response times vary. Scope, source fees and anticipated timing are confirmed for the actual matter.

Answers for Your Request

Does the central Florida UCC registry replace county property research?

No. The central registry handles its UCC records. Recorded property instruments and requested local-department responses are different sources and must be defined separately.

Are all Florida permit or occupancy records available online?

Availability depends on the authority, record type and date. Hillsborough County’s instructions, for example, identify different retrieval routes for different periods. Confirm the responsible authority and required document.
