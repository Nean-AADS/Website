Georgia Public-Record Research: UCC, Recorded Liens and Court Sources

Distinguish GSCCCA record indexes from the specific Georgia courts needed for litigation research, and define the names, counties, period and copies for each request.

Georgia’s GSCCCA UCC index offers name, filing-number, county/date and secured-party searches. Its notice effective 1 November 2026 requires UCC-11 information requests to be submitted electronically through Certified Search Request.

STATE REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

Start with the Source Map

Use this guide to prepare the request. It maps source types and questions to confirm; it is not a statewide completeness certificate or a legal determination of required scope.

Treat Each Index as a Defined Source

GSCCCA lists separate UCC, real-estate, lien and plat search resources, including a certified UCC search request route. Select the index and requested deliverable explicitly. An index result and a certified response or underlying instrument are different records.

Name the Georgia Court Type

Georgia’s judicial guidance describes several trial-court classes. Superior, state and magistrate court sources can involve different records. The request should identify the court classes and counties chosen by the reviewing team; the phrase “county litigation search” alone leaves those choices open.

Confirm Coverage and Obtain the Record

The judicial directory helps identify the relevant court contact. County custody, available indexes, historical coverage, images and certification requirements need confirmation for the actual request. Federal district and bankruptcy records are a separate research path.

Request Checklist

  • Each subject name and the requested GSCCCA index, certification and copy requirements.
  • County, property identifiers, instrument references and title depth where relevant.
  • The actual superior, state, magistrate or other court sources requested.
  • Civil case categories, period, pending/closed instructions and any known case numbers.
  • Separate federal/bankruptcy scope, desired documents, matter reference and deadline.

Example Request

“For [subject] in [Georgia county], please quote the specified GSCCCA UCC/lien research and civil litigation in [named court classes]. Include [period/status categories], located case references and [documents/copies]. Separately confirm any federal or bankruptcy research. Identify the indexes and courts actually searched.”

Make the Result Assessable

Ask the deliverable to identify the exact names and property, sources and criteria used, covered period or through date, located references, supporting copies and any outstanding items. Online coverage, authentication, historical records and source response times vary. Scope, source fees and anticipated timing are confirmed for the actual matter.

Answers for Your Request

Does a GSCCCA recorded-lien search replace civil litigation research?

No. Specify the recorded indexes and the court case sources separately. They answer different record questions and may have different coverage and deliverables.

Which Georgia courts should be included?

Your reviewing team should identify the appropriate court classes and counties for the matter. AADS confirms the requested sources and deliverables; one court search should not be represented as every Georgia court.
