Appointment and Change Coordination

AADS supports registered agent appointment and change requests as part of its corporate services. Identify every state and entity involved so the appointment, acceptance and any filing steps can be confirmed before proceeding.

Prepare Your Request

  • Legal entity name, entity type, formation state and file number if known.
  • Each state requiring an appointment or change.
  • Whether this is a new entity, a qualification or a replacement of an existing agent.
  • Authorized end-client contact details, desired effective date and related filing instructions.

Confirm How Notices Will Reach the Right People

Before an appointment begins, confirm the responsible agent, accepted service scope, designated client recipients and notice-forwarding arrangements. Keep those contacts current. Do not rely on an appointment request as evidence that the change has been accepted or filed.

Separate the Related Work

Annual reports, entity amendments, certificates of good standing and other filings are separately defined requests. Receiving service of process for an appointed entity is also different from arranging delivery of legal papers to another party. Ask for the specific work required.

Answers for Your Next Request

What information is needed for a registered agent appointment or change?

Provide the entity’s legal name, formation state, file number if known, each state requiring an appointment, and an authorized end-client contact. Identify whether the entity is new or changing agents. Appointment acceptance, consent and filing requirements are confirmed before proceeding.

Is a registered agent appointment the same as arranging service of process?

No. An agent appointment concerns the designated recipient for notices and service of process for an entity. Arranging delivery of legal papers to another party is a separate request with different instructions and availability.

Are annual reports and ongoing monitoring included with an agent appointment?

Do not assume they are included. Confirm the appointment, notice-handling arrangements, any annual-report filing work and any separately available follow-up services in the agreed scope.
