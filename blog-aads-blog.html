In an effort to communicate better with our clients and share important news and analysis from our industry, AADS will be releasing a newsletter and a blog from this point forward. In this blog, we’ll give you a brief introduction to the services, advantages, and company culture that have led to our success.

AADS Services

At AADS, we have over 40 years of combined experience in the field of public records. We support the real estate industry, lenders, attorneys, and businesses nationwide with document abstracting, document searches, document delivery, and process service. We deal with time-sensitive projects, and our key to success has been our rapid turnaround time—usually between 24 to 48 hours.

Our services include:

Searches

AADS helps clients mitigate risk with state and federal searches, including bankruptcy, criminal records, civil records, and court judgments. We also perform thorough lien searches on individuals, corporations, and properties.

UCC, Corporate and registered agent services

AADS’ serves as a registered agent, using our research team to perform UCC and lien searches. We handle document delivery and process of service as well.

Document abstracting

We protect lenders, corporations, and real estate professionals with thorough examination and abstraction of legal, financial, and public records. Our document abstraction services increase client efficiency and productivity, all while providing peace of mind.

Process servicing and skip tracing

We have a licensed team of process servers and skip tracers who work diligently, making sure court summons and other legal documents are delivered quickly and legally to the correct individuals.

Our Place in the Industry

Technological advances in the last 15 years have altered courthouse services to the very core, and we’ve taken advantage of every opportunity to decrease turnaround time and increase efficiency. Another aspect of our business we constantly strive to improve is our customer service. Our research team provides the same dedication and thoroughness on every project and shows the same respect to every client.
