Certificate of Good Standing, Online Status & Certified Copies: What to Order

REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 9 NOVEMBER 2026

Start here

Match the document to the receiving party’s requirement. A certificate of good standing, an online status result and a certified copy serve different needs.

Ask for the requirement before ordering

“Please send the company documents” leaves important choices open. Obtain the wording from the lender, law firm, authority or other recipient. Confirm the entity, issuing jurisdiction, document type, certification and date requirements before requesting production.

Delaware provides a useful example: its online-status guidance distinguishes an online status result from an official certificate of good standing. It also explains that a status-and-history result does not supply images of the underlying filed documents. Other states may use different names and processes, so use the relevant issuing office’s instructions.

Separate the deliverables

Requested itemWhat to clarify
Online entity or status resultDoes the recipient accept a database result, or require an official certificate of good standing?
Certificate of good standing (or the issuing office’s equivalent)Which issuing state, certificate of good standing form and issuance-date requirement apply?
Copy of a filed documentWhich formation document, amendment, filing date or document number is needed?
Certified copyWhich underlying document must be certified by the office holding the record?

Prepare a complete corporate document request

  1. Confirm the entity. Provide the exact legal name and file number if available. A similar company name is not enough to identify the intended record confidently.
  2. Name the jurisdiction. Specify the issuing state and distinguish a formation-state requirement from a requirement involving another registration.
  3. Specify the item. Identify whether you need a certificate of good standing, a plain copy or a certified copy, and any requested version or historical filing.
  4. Keep the recipient’s conditions. Include any document-age, original-format or delivery condition rather than assuming every certificate of good standing is accepted.
  5. Identify international use. If the document is going abroad, provide the destination country and recipient instructions so any separate authentication route can be reviewed.

Illustrative request wording

“The receiving party requires an official certificate of good standing for the entity identified below, issued within its stated date window. Please confirm the available form, fees, anticipated timing and delivery format before ordering.”

AADS can coordinate the specified records and filings. The issuing authority determines what it can issue, and the receiving party determines whether the supplied item satisfies its requirement.
