What You Receive

Your confirmed scope identifies the deliverables. Depending on the request, these can include:

Requested source documents
The official record or certified copy included in the agreed retrieval and international-use request.
Certification output
The apostille, authentication or legalization work available through the confirmed origin and destination route.
Document and route identification
The document, issuing authority and receiving-party requirements used to prepare the request.
Delivery and outstanding items
The agreed electronic or original-document delivery arrangements, with pending steps or unavailable items identified.

Retrieval, translations where arranged, government processing, consular steps and original-document delivery are separately scoped and quoted.

Document Support for International Use

  • Retrieval of specified official or certified documents where available.
  • Apostille coordination through the relevant issuing authority.
  • Authentication and legalization coordination for the confirmed destination.
  • Review of stated recipient requirements and requested document format.
  • Delivery and return-shipping arrangements within the agreed service.

Prepare Your Request

  • Document type, issuing authority and state or federal origin.
  • Country of use and the receiving party’s written requirements.
  • Whether the available document is an original, certified copy or other copy.
  • Any translation, delivery or document-age requirement.
  • Date needed and return-delivery instructions.

Clear scope before work begins

Confirm the required document preparation and processing route before proceeding. Authentication addresses the official signature or seal; the document’s contents are assessed separately.

From Your Request to Delivery

  2. Confirm the jurisdiction-specific scope, fees, expected delivery format and anticipated timing.
  3. Coordinate the agreed research, filing or document work and review the returned material.
  4. Deliver the agreed work with any outstanding items or source limits identified.

Answers for Your Next Request

What is needed for an apostille or legalization request?

Identify the document, issuing authority, destination country, receiving party’s instructions and date needed. These details help establish the processing route and whether document retrieval or another certification step is needed.

How long does a search or document request take?

Timing depends on the service, jurisdiction, source availability and the records required. Share your deadline at the start. Anticipated timing and any available rush option are confirmed for the actual request.

What determines the quote and document copy fees?

The quote depends on the names or properties, jurisdictions, record categories, copies, certifications and any rush requirements. Confirm the service charges and applicable government or document fees in the quote before proceeding.
