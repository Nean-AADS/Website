Are you sick of so-so customer service? Juggling public records research vendors? AADS can help you clarify legal and financial risk and make 2017 a successful and stress-free year. Here’s how.

We Mean Business with our Customer Service

We don’t skimp on quality, and we don’t take customer service lightly. Our commitment to real, people-focused customer service and real process innovations have made us a top choice for a growing number of companies and industries.

We Serve Your Industry

AADS invests in local experts that perform on-site search and retrieval of the documents essential to your industry, your clients and your business. We have been serving clients and individuals in the real estate, legal, corporate, transaction processing industries, and factoring industries since 2002.
Our services include, but are not limited to: CORPORATE SERVICES, UCC LIEN SEARCHES, RECORDED AND UNRECORDED LIEN SEARCHES, REAL PROPERTY TITLE AND MUNICIPAL LIEN SEARCHES, CRIMINAL SEARCHES, CIVIL LITIGATION, BACKGROUND CHECKS, and FILINGS.
