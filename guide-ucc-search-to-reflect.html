Start with the Filing Evidence

Provide the office’s filing acknowledgment, financing statement number, exact debtor name and filing date. Identify whether the request concerns an initial statement or a later amendment. Ask for the particular record or indexed-search result your reviewer needs.

Request the Search Separately

A search to reflect is a check for the identified filing in the filing office’s searchable records. Specify the office, exact search name, certification, copies and required through date. Indexing and the scope of the response depend on the office.

A Washington Example

Washington’s rules describe a search requested with an initial financing statement being conducted when relevant records through that filing date can be retrieved. The state’s search-response rules identify the search name, through date and returned filings. This is an example of one jurisdiction’s process, not a nationwide timing promise.

A Useful Request

“Please obtain a search to reflect financing statement [number] filed in [office] on [date] under [exact debtor name]. Include [certification/copies required], identify the through date and return the filing acknowledgment with the result.”

Review the Record, Then Resolve Questions

Compare the requested filing number and debtor information with the response. Flag an unavailable record or mismatch for follow-up rather than treating it as proof of legal invalidity. A filing acknowledgment and indexed search do not decide priority, perfection or enforceability.
