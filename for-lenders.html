Prepare Your Request

  • Exact borrower and related-party names, entity types and formation jurisdictions.
  • The states, counties, courts and collateral-related sources selected by your reviewing team.
  • Required UCC certifications, search through dates and supporting copies.
  • Any filing acknowledgment to be checked by a search to reflect, plus the deadline and matter reference.

Example Request

“For [borrower] and [related names], please quote the agreed state/county lien searches, litigation in [courts] and bankruptcy research within [scope]. Include the required search certificates, supporting copies and source dates. Please separately quote a search to reflect filing [number] in [office].”

What the Handoff Should Make Clear

Clearly identified records, sources and search dates give your legal and credit teams the evidence needed to assess the findings.

Answers for Your Request

Can the request cover several borrowers or jurisdictions?

Yes. List every subject and jurisdiction separately and specify the record categories and copies for each. AADS confirms the combined scope, fees and anticipated timing.

Is a search to reflect automatically included with a filing?

Confirm it separately. A filing acknowledgment records the office’s submission result, while a search to reflect checks the identified filing in indexed records through the reported date.
