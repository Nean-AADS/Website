Washington Public-Record Research: UCC, Entity and County Sources

Route Washington UCC requests to the Department of Licensing, entity records to the Secretary of State and county or court records to their responsible custodians.

Washington UCC filing and UCC-11 searches are handled by the Department of Licensing, while business-entity records are handled by the Secretary of State. Its UCC forms distinguish information requests from amendments and information statements.

STATE REQUEST-PREPARATION GUIDE · 10 NOVEMBER 2026

Start with the Source Map

Use this guide to prepare the request. It maps source types and questions to confirm; it is not a statewide completeness certificate or a legal determination of required scope.

Which Source Answers Which Question?

Research componentResponsible sourceDefine before ordering
Central UCC recordsWashington Department of LicensingDebtor names, filing references, search type and copies
Business entity recordsSecretary of State Corporations and Charities Filing System (CCFS)Entity name or UBI, filing history and the required certificate or copy
Recorded property documentsThe responsible county recording officeProperty, instrument references, owner period and copies
Court case information and documentsWashington court search resources and the court of recordCourt, names, period, case number and underlying documents

Use the Correct State Office

Washington’s UCC filing and search guidance is published by the Department of Licensing. Corporate entity records are maintained through the Secretary of State’s CCFS. These are separate systems; an entity-status lookup is not a UCC search.

Keep the Acknowledgment and Search Result Together

Provide the filing acknowledgment and number, together with any separately requested search-to-reflect requirements, so your reviewing team receives the appropriate evidence.

Check County and Court Coverage

Thurston County’s recording guidance is one example of a county property source and distinguishes indexed information from documents available online. Washington Courts also explains that its name/case search is reference information; the official case record and document copies are held by the court of record.

Request Checklist

  • Exact debtor names and the Department of Licensing UCC request type.
  • Legal entity name, UBI if known and the exact CCFS document or status certificate needed.
  • County, property identifiers and any known recording references.
  • Court and case information, period, dockets and copies needed.
  • Filing acknowledgment, search through-date requirement, matter reference and deadline.

Example Request

“Please quote a Washington Department of Licensing UCC search for [exact name], with [certification/copies], and separately retrieve [entity document] from CCFS. For [county/property], include [specified records]. For court work, identify [court/period] and the documents required. Keep each source and through date distinct.”

Make the Result Assessable

Ask the deliverable to identify the exact names and property, sources and criteria used, covered period or through date, located references, supporting copies and any outstanding items. Online coverage, authentication, historical records and source response times vary. Scope, source fees and anticipated timing are confirmed for the actual matter.

Answers for Your Request

Does Washington’s Secretary of State handle the central UCC search described here?

Washington’s Department of Licensing provides the central UCC filing and search service. The Secretary of State’s CCFS is the separate source for corporate entity records.

Does the Washington name-and-case search provide the full official case file?

Washington Courts states that the search is reference information. The court of record maintains the official case record, and requested copies should be obtained through the appropriate court process.
